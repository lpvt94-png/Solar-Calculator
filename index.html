Why Go Solar in 2026?

Take advantage of historic incentives and lock in your energy costs for decades

30% Federal Tax Credit

The Investment Tax Credit (ITC) gives you 30% back on your solar system cost. Available through 2032, but don't wait—it decreases to 26% in 2033.

Protection from Rising Rates

Utility rates increase 3-5% annually. Lock in your energy costs today and save more as rates climb over the next 25 years.

Increase Home Value

Studies show solar panels increase home value by 4.1% on average. That's $20,000+ for a $500,000 home—often more than the system cost.

Environmental Impact

The average home solar system offsets 100+ tons of CO₂ over 25 years—equivalent to planting 2,500+ trees or not driving 250,000 miles.

Energy Independence

Generate your own clean electricity. Add battery storage for backup power during outages and true energy independence.

Low Maintenance

Solar panels have no moving parts and require minimal maintenance. Most come with 25-year warranties and can last 30-40 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this solar calculator?

Our calculator uses US national averages for electricity rates ($0.16/kWh), solar installation costs ($3.00/watt), and peak sun hours (4 hours/day). Your actual savings may vary based on your location, utility rates, roof characteristics, and available local incentives. For a precise estimate, request quotes from local installers.

What is the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit?

The Investment Tax Credit (ITC) allows you to deduct 30% of your solar system cost from your federal taxes. For a $30,000 system, that's $9,000 back. This incentive is available through 2032, then decreases to 26% in 2033 and 22% in 2034.

How long do solar panels last?

Most solar panels come with 25-year performance warranties and can last 30-40 years. They typically lose about 0.5% efficiency per year, so after 25 years, they'll still operate at 87-88% of their original capacity.

Do I need to replace my roof before installing solar?

If your roof is in good condition with 10+ years of life remaining, you can install solar. If your roof needs replacement soon, it's best to do it before solar installation. Many homeowners bundle both projects together.

Can I finance my solar system?

Yes! Most installers offer financing options including solar loans (often with $0 down), home equity loans, HELOC, or PACE financing. Many homeowners find that their monthly loan payment is less than their previous electric bill.

What happens if I sell my home?

Owned solar systems typically increase home value by 4.1% on average. The solar system transfers with the home sale, and studies show homes with solar sell faster and for more money than comparable non-solar homes.
